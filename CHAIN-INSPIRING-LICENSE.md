## 5. Ecosystem Allocation (token airdrop)

### 5.1 Allocation obligation

If an Inspired Project issues a Project Token, You must irrevocably allocate and deliver to the Designated Recipient, without charge, no less than the **Allocation Rate** of the Issued Supply of each Project Token (the **"Ecosystem Allocation"**). The default Allocation Rate is **0.01% (one ten-thousandth)**, but an applicable Project Schedule may set a different rate under Section 4.8. Calculate the Ecosystem Allocation in the smallest transferable on-chain denomination and round any fraction up to the next whole unit.

If separate obligations apply to more than one Licensor or Project Schedule, You must calculate and satisfy each Allocation Rate separately for its respective Designated Recipient unless the affected Licensors agree otherwise in a signed or otherwise verifiable writing.

### 5.2 Initial delivery

You must complete the initial Ecosystem Allocation no later than thirty days after the later of:

(a) the first public distribution of the Project Token; and

(b) the date the Project Token first becomes transferable by an unaffiliated public holder.

### 5.3 Supply increases and anti-avoidance

Whenever Issued Supply increases after the initial delivery, You must deliver an additional amount within thirty days so that the cumulative Ecosystem Allocation remains at least the applicable Allocation Rate of Issued Supply. You must not use multiple issuers, affiliated entities, token migrations, denomination changes, sidechains, replacement tokens, burns, vesting arrangements, or substantially equivalent instruments to evade or reduce the Ecosystem Allocation.

### 5.4 Economic and governance treatment

Tokens in the Ecosystem Allocation must be of the same class and carry the same per-token economic and governance rights as the corresponding Project Tokens distributed to unaffiliated public participants. They must be fully paid and must not carry recipient-specific vesting, lockup, clawback, or transfer restrictions, except as required by applicable law.

### 5.5 Delivery, records, and fees

You must pay ordinary network and delivery fees and keep publicly verifiable records sufficient to confirm the calculation, allocation, and delivery. Delivery of Project Tokens does not grant the Designated Recipient control over, partnership in, or responsibility for the Inspired Project.

### 5.6 Missing address or legal restriction

If no valid Designated Recipient address or mechanism is available when delivery is due, You must:

(a) make reasonable documented efforts to obtain one;

(b) reserve the full Ecosystem Allocation in a segregated, publicly verifiable on-chain address or mechanism; and

(c) deliver it promptly once valid delivery instructions are available.

If a transfer would violate applicable law, You must not make the unlawful transfer. You must give the Licensor a written explanation, continue to reserve the full allocation in a verifiable manner, and cooperate in good faith on a lawful delivery structure. A cash, stablecoin, or other substitute satisfies this Section only if the Licensor agrees in a signed or otherwise verifiable writing.
