Become a Publisher

Publishers are the heartbeat of Congrid. You register your site and prove ownership by placing a simple badge link on your homepage. In return, you help build an open discovery graph — and you can earn protocol rewards.

Why join?

  • Fair discovery: your content gets found based on meaning, not opaque ad-driven ranking.
  • Referral traffic: your URL can appear on similar sites’ Congrid blocks.
  • Rewards: availability + verification consistency can qualify for payouts.
  • Ownership: you keep your site — you’re not uploading into a walled garden.

What you need

  • A website with a public homepage at /
  • A wallet address in Congrid format (congrid1...)
  • Ability to paste a tiny HTML snippet into your homepage template

Step 1 — Choose how to provide your wallet address

You can either connect Keplr/Leap or paste an address manually. Both paths are supported.

Option A — Connect wallet (recommended)

Connect to auto-fill your address from Keplr/Leap.

Wallet: Not connected

Option B — Paste address manually

If you prefer not to connect any extension, you can paste your congrid1... address directly in Step 2.

Keplr setup guide (manual chain add)

  1. Install/open Keplr browser extension.
  2. Open this page and click Connect wallet. Congrid will try to auto-suggest chain config.
  3. If Keplr asks for approval, approve chain + account access.
  4. If auto-suggest is blocked, add network manually in Keplr using:
  • Chain ID: {{.WalletConfig.ChainID}}
  • RPC: {{.WalletConfig.RPC}}
  • REST: http://127.0.0.1:1317 (adjust to your node REST endpoint)
  • Address prefix: grid
  • Base denom: {{if .WalletConfig.FeeDenom}}{{.WalletConfig.FeeDenom}}{{else}}ucongrid{{end}}
  • Display denom: CONGRID (6 decimals)

After adding the network, create/import account in Keplr and copy its congrid1... address.

Step 2 — Fill in your wallet + website to prepare registration

Enter your website domain and owner wallet address below. We’ll generate a ready-to-use registration command and badge snippet for your site.

Note: the badge link href must be exactly {{.BaseURL}} (no query string or fragment) to pass strict verification rules.

Step 3 — Update your website, then submit registration

Paste the generated badge snippet into your homepage template and deploy your site. If you connected Keplr/Leap, you can register directly in-browser (no local CLI needed).

Wallet mode: click the button below and approve the transaction in your wallet extension. The connected wallet address must match the owner wallet from Step 2.

Prefer CLI? You can still run the generated command manually as fallback.

Step 4 — Wait for verifier checks

Airdrop remains available as an optional starter balance for other on-chain actions (for example marketplace and slot operations), but it is not required for publisher registration itself.

Verifiers periodically fetch your homepage and confirm the badge link is present and matches your registered owner wallet. Keep the badge intact and your site online to stay eligible.
