Become a Publisher

Publishers are the heartbeat of Congrid. You register your site and prove ownership by placing a simple badge link on your homepage. In return, you help build an open discovery graph — and you can earn protocol rewards.

Why join?

  • Fair discovery: your content gets found based on meaning, not opaque ad-driven ranking.
  • Referral traffic: your URL can appear on similar sites’ Congrid blocks.
  • Rewards: availability + verification consistency can qualify for payouts.
  • Ownership: you keep your site — you’re not uploading into a walled garden.

What you need

  • A website with a public homepage at /
  • A wallet address (bech32) that will own the publisher registration
  • Ability to add a tiny HTML snippet to your homepage template

Step 1 — Add the badge to your homepage

Your homepage must include a link to the Congrid official website, and the link must wrap an image. The image URL must point to {{.BaseURL}}/badge.png and include publisher and wallet parameters. (These parameters bind the badge to your domain and wallet.) 

<a href="{{.BaseURL}}">
  <img
    alt="Verified by Congrid"
    src="{{.BaseURL}}/badge.png?publisher=example.com&wallet=<your-bech32-owner-address>"
  />
</a>

Note: the href must be exactly {{.BaseURL}} (no query string or fragment) to pass strict verification rules.

Step 2 — Register your domain

After the badge is live, broadcast the on-chain registration transaction. The protocol locks your primary domain (e.g. example.com) to prevent subdomain squatting. 

./content-grid-d publisher register example.com \
  --from <key-or-address> \
  --metadata-uri <optional-link>

Step 3 — Keep it verified

Verifiers periodically fetch your homepage and confirm the badge link is present and matches your registered owner wallet. Keep the badge intact and your site online to stay eligible.

Want help onboarding? If you share your domain and owner address, we can generate a ready-to-paste snippet customized for your site.
