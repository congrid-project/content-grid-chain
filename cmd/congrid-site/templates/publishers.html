Get your address with a third-party wallet

Recommended wallets

  • Keplr — browser extension and mobile wallet.
  • Leap — browser extension and mobile wallet.

Address + key basics

  1. Install Keplr or Leap from the official site, then create a new wallet or import an existing one.
  2. Copy your public Congrid address (congrid1...) from the wallet when this page asks for owner wallet.
  3. Your recovery phrase or private key stays in the wallet. Save it offline and never paste it into Congrid.
  4. If you use the browser extension, the connect button below can read your public address and ask the wallet to sign for you.

Step 1 — Connect wallet (optional but recommended)

Connect Keplr/Leap to auto-fill your owner address and register on-chain directly in browser.

Wallet: Not connected

Step 2 — Enter domain + owner wallet

We will generate: (1) a CLI register command and (2) an exact badge snippet.

Verification is strict: badge link href must be exactly {{.BaseURL}}. Keep the badge on homepage / and do not change the owner wallet without re-registration.

Step 3 — Register on-chain

Browser wallet mode (recommended): click button below and approve transaction in Keplr/Leap.

CLI mode: use generated command and replace <local-key-name> with your local key alias.

Step 4 — Stay healthy

Verifiers continuously check your homepage badge and owner wallet binding. Keep your site online and the badge intact to remain eligible for network programs.
