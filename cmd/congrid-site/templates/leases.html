Public lease ledger for publishers and advertisers. Use this board to copy verified embed snippets and audit lease state.

Usage checklist

  1. Find your active lease row by slot_id / lease_id.
  2. Copy the generated embed snippet exactly.
  3. Publish snippet to the agreed placement on your page.
  4. Keep attributes unchanged during lease term.

Integrity requirements

  • Wrapper must include data-congrid-slot-id = slot_id.
  • Wrapper must include data-congrid-lease = lease_id.
  • Target URL must match lease contract.
  • Do not mutate IDs or remove tracking attributes.
