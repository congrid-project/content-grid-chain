What Congrid does

Semantic indexing

Workers fetch pages and compute embeddings off-chain (pluggable embedder). Compact similarity signatures help with de-duplication and diversity.

Deterministic stake-weighted assignment

Tasks are assigned using a deterministic, block-hash/drand-seeded mechanism with stake-weighted random selection — no trusted coordinator.

On-chain incentives

The chain routes rewards to the participants who keep the network healthy: publishers, workers, verifiers, and community.

Open participation

Anyone can publish. Anyone can verify. The goal is a durable, community-owned index that resists capture and censorship.
