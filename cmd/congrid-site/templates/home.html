What Congrid does

Publisher registry

Publishers register a primary domain and anchor ownership by hosting the Congrid badge. This registry powers trusted attribution and discovery.

Verifier assignment

Bonded verifiers receive assignment windows and submit commit/reveal results to keep the registry accurate.

On-chain incentives

The chain routes rewards to participants who keep the network healthy: publishers, verifiers, and community.

Open participation

Anyone can publish. Anyone can verify. The goal is a durable, community-owned index that resists capture and censorship.
