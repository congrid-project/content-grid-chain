Publisher + Verifier Guides

Everything here is focused on joining Congrid as a publisher or a verifier.

Become a Publisher

Publishers join Congrid to receive free backlinks from similar websites and ongoing Congrid token rewards.

  1. Prepare your domain and Congrid wallet.
  2. Add the Congrid badge to your homepage.
  3. Register the domain and keep the homepage verified.
Open publisher guide →

Become a Verifier

Verifiers bond on-chain, validate publisher status, and earn a larger share of Congrid token rewards for accurate work.

  1. Run the official Linux/macOS native installer.
  2. Fund the verifier address printed by the installer.
  3. Bond on-chain and monitor the managed verifier services.
Open installer and verifier guide →
